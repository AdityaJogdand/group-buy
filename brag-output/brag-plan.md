# Group Buy — launch video plan

**What it is:** strangers pool together for a seller's bulk price; PayPal holds each buyer's max and everyone pays one lower group price at the deadline, or nobody pays.
**For:** anyone buying something that gets cheaper by the ten (courses, software seats, gear).
**Sets it apart:** the money is *held, not spent* until the group is real (PayPal authorize → capture or void), and you can join by just asking an AI.
**Most impressive claim:** "Every buyer lowers the price." And the guarantee: charged once, or released, never more than your max.
**Visual hook:** a price falling as seats fill: $350 → $300 → $270.
**Real UI shown:** the hero copy, a live group card (real headphones photo, real tiers $350 / $300 at 10 / $270 at 25), the hold lifecycle cards, the Ask AI panel.
**Tone:** `polished` (premium, modern, sleek): the site's own monochrome look, long holds, soft dips through black, one accent of motion per scene.
**Share caption:** "Every buyer lowers the price. Group Buy pools strangers for the bulk price; PayPal holds your max, and you pay the group price, or nothing."

## Identity
